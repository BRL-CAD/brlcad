== NAME

STEP - ISO 10303 product-data exchange and BRL-CAD support

[[overview]]
== OVERVIEW

STEP is ISO 10303, _Industrial automation systems and integration -- Product
data representation and exchange_. It is a family of standards rather than a
single geometry format. Three layers are especially relevant to BRL-CAD:

* ISO 10303-11 defines EXPRESS, the schema language.
* ISO 10303-21 defines the clear-text exchange encoding commonly called a
  STEP or Part 21 file.
* An application protocol (AP) defines the permitted product, assembly,
  geometry, presentation, and metadata entities.

The *.step* or *.stp* suffix alone does not identify the data model. A Part 21
file's *FILE_SCHEMA* header selects its schema. *step-g*(1) inspects that
declaration and loads an import plugin; *g-step*(1) selects an export plugin
explicitly.

[[protocols]]
== SUPPORTED APPLICATION PROTOCOLS

When the corresponding schemas are enabled at build time, BRL-CAD provides
one importer and exporter host for these mechanical APs:

[cols="1,2,3"]
|===
|Plugin key |Part 21 schema family |BRL-CAD coverage

|*ap203*
|`CONFIG_CONTROL_DESIGN` (ISO 10303-203 edition 1)
|Mechanical shape, product structure, and selected administration; the default export schema

|*ap203e2*
|`AP203_CONFIGURATION_CONTROLLED_3D_DESIGN_OF_MECHANICAL_PARTS_AND_ASSEMBLIES_MIM_LF`
|Edition-specific mechanical geometry, exact supported sweeps, presentation and metadata, and optional native CSG

|*ap214*
|`AUTOMOTIVE_DESIGN` (AP214 edition 3)
|Mechanical geometry and assemblies, selected presentation and metadata, exact supported sweeps, and optional native CSG

|*ap242e1* through *ap242e4* (`ap242` aliases e4 on export)
|`AP242_MANAGED_MODEL_BASED_3D_ENGINEERING_MIM_LF`
|Edition-isolated bindings with shared mechanical conversion, tessellated import, selected PMI import, and optional native CSG
|===

BRL-CAD implements an interoperable subset of each AP, not every entity or
conformance class in the standard. Unsupported and malformed represented
content is reported explicitly.

[[schema_selection]]
== FILE_SCHEMA SELECTION

A sole unknown schema or a header naming multiple supported AP families is
rejected rather than guessed. One supported AP plus an unknown companion
identifier is accepted with a warning. IFC identifiers are recognized, but
the user is directed to the separate *ifc-g*(1) converter.

AP242 editions 1 through 4 share a long-form schema identifier but do not have
identical entity sets or physical attribute layouts. Automatic import tries
e4 through e1 when an attempted layout cannot produce usable geometry. A file
using only a compatible common subset may remain ambiguous; specify
*step-g --schema ap242eN* when the authoring edition is known.

Interim identifiers `CONFIGURATION_CONTROL_3D_DESIGN_MIM_LF`,
`CCD_CLA_GVP_AST`, and `CCD_CLA_GVP_AST_ASD` select AP203e2. Verified headers
combining `CONFIG_CONTROL_DESIGN` with geometric-validation-property or
shape-appearance-layer MIM identifiers also use that binding. These are import
compatibility paths, not additional export formats.

Part 21 Edition 1 *SCOPE* and *ENDSCOPE* groups are accepted by the active
reader. Scoped instances and exported roots participate in the same schema
selection, reference resolution, and conversion policy as ordinary DATA
instances.

[[geometry]]
== GEOMETRY COVERAGE

=== B-reps, surfaces, and wireframes

The importers cover advanced and faceted B-reps, B-reps with voids, manifold
surface models, shell-based surface models, mapped items, solid replicas,
geometric sets, and supported wireframe representations. Geometry normally
becomes a BRL-CAD *brep* unless an exact native mapping is available. Units,
representation uncertainty, and placements are applied during conversion.

Open boundaries in a shell-based surface model may be intended sheet geometry
or construction content. They are imported by default. The
*step-g --skip-open-shells* option filters only *OPEN_SHELL* boundaries from
those surface models; closed surface boundaries and solid B-rep topology
remain enabled.

Bounded geometric sets retain exact curves as B-reps and Cartesian points as
*pnts* objects. Mixed sets become combinations. Product-owned constructive
geometry planes become *datum* planes; placement axes remain coordinate
frames rather than standalone geometry.

STEP and OpenNURBS use different spline-knot storage conventions. The importer
validates the expanded STEP knot vector and removes only the two extra
endpoint knots OpenNURBS does not store. Closed unclamped surfaces are
normalized only when a dense before-and-after audit remains within the source
uncertainty.

=== Swept solids

AP203e2, AP214, and AP242 share exact conversion for supported
*EXTRUDED_AREA_SOLID*, *REVOLVED_AREA_SOLID*, *EXTRUDED_FACE_SOLID*,
*REVOLVED_FACE_SOLID*, and *SWEPT_DISK_SOLID* entities. Area and face sweeps
become exact B-reps. Profiles may include inner void loops.

A linear swept disk becomes a native cylinder or cylinder-difference region.
A direct circular sweep becomes a full or partial toroidal B-rep, including
an optional inner void. Other nonlinear directrices are reported rather than
approximated. Trimmed and drafted face-sweep subtypes are not treated as their
base sweeps because that would discard termination, offset, or draft
semantics.

=== AP242 tessellated geometry

Triangulated faces, strips, fans, surface sets, shells, and solids use a direct
BOT path. Coordinates, indices, degeneracy, normals, and winding are validated.
Tessellated solid intent produces a volume BOT only when the triangles are
closed and consistently oriented; incomplete input remains a diagnosed
surface BOT. Tessellated point, curve, wire, and geometric sets do not yet
have a direct mapping.

[[assemblies]]
== PRODUCT STRUCTURE AND ASSEMBLIES

Product definitions become combinations and occurrences retain rigid
placement matrices. AP203 import supports the three assembly-shape styles in
Recommended Practice 8:

* referenced shape using NAUO, CDSR, and a transforming representation
  relationship;
* mapped shape using *REPRESENTATION_MAP* and *MAPPED_ITEM*; and
* replicated shape using product usage with an identity occurrence.

Multi-level assemblies and repeated transformed instances are supported.
Higher-usage paths, quantities, promissory usages, alternate products, and
usage substitutes are retained in the structured report even when they do not
create geometry. Transforming representation relationships become assembly
edges only when product-usage context gives them occurrence semantics;
otherwise they remain placed secondary geometry of the same product.

On export, union-only combinations become assemblies. Nested product identity,
repeated rigid occurrences, and imported representation layering are
preserved. Scaling is reported because this product mapping cannot express it.
A non-union Boolean tree is not recast as an assembly union; it requires the
optional native CSG path or future evaluated B-rep export.

[[csg]]
== NATIVE CSG

Native CSG import is automatic for supported AP203e2, AP214, and AP242
entities. Export is opt-in with *g-step --native-csg* because many downstream
systems interoperate more reliably with B-reps. The complete expression is
validated before it is attached to a product.

Union, intersection, and difference map directly; XOR is lowered to an
equivalent expression. BRL-CAD NOT and guard/no-op nodes have no lossless
finite STEP encoding and therefore fail instead of changing model meaning.

AP203e2 and AP214 share sphere, block, right angular wedge, torus, circular
cone, circular cylinder, and planar half-space mappings. AP242 also permits
exact ellipsoid, eccentric cone, rectangular pyramid, tetrahedron, and convex
hexahedron mappings. A finite unsupported primitive may use its B-rep as a
leaf inside an otherwise preserved tree. Cyclide export remains unavailable
because BRL-CAD does not provide the additional exact parameterization needed
for a safe mapping.

[[pmi]]
== AP242 PMI AND MODEL-BASED DEFINITION

AP242 import retains the connected semantic, presentation, association, and
dependency PMI graph. Each record keeps its Part 21 value, complex-component
order, references, product owner, category, and native-conversion disposition.

Exactly resolved datum points, linear or cylindrical axes, and planar
references become native BRL-CAD *datum* objects with source and feature
links. Multiple candidates must agree within tolerance; distinct parallel
planes are not replaced by an invented median plane. Tessellated graphical
curves reached through an annotation plane and draughting callout become
model-space *annot* objects using the authored basis and placement.

Graphical outlines do not prove semantic text. Glyph strokes are retained as
curves and are not claimed to reconstruct their original font, tolerance, or
dimension. Unsupported datum targets, leaders, fills, symbols, and
semantic-only content remain in the graph with explicit dispositions.
Malformed presentation makes permissive import partial and strict import
transactional. PMI export is not yet implemented.

[[metadata]]
== METADATA ROUND TRIP

=== Storage and object attributes

Stable values are stored as `step:` attributes on their owning products or
occurrences. Product fields include original name and identifier,
descriptions, revision, and definition identity. Presentation fields retain
colour, transparency, style name, and layers. Source representation contexts
record length and angle factors, normalized units, and uncertainty as
provenance rather than automatic future export instructions.

Reference-linked graphs without a direct object equivalent are encoded in the
portable, versioned `_STEP_METADATA` binary object. Locator attributes are
written on both that object and *_GLOBAL*. For compatibility, a modest graph
is also mirrored as directly editable *_GLOBAL* attributes; large graphs use
only the locator there to avoid poorly scaling attribute operations.
*g-step* reads the binary object first and merges compatible global attributes.

Source Part 21 identifiers are internal graph anchors only. The exporter
allocates new instance numbers and remaps every supported reference after
validating that its target still has one emitted counterpart.

=== Presentation, materials, properties, and units

AP203e2, AP214, and AP242 share colour, transparency, layer, material, and
typed property round trips. A native BRL-CAD colour overrides retained STEP
colour on export. Product properties and material properties remain distinct.
Supported values include descriptive text, Cartesian coordinates, and typed
numeric measures.

Numeric properties retain measure type, seven dimensional exponents, a
normalized label, and a recursive unit graph. SI, context-dependent,
conversion-based, and derived units can be re-authored, including conversion
units nested in derived units. The graph takes precedence over a convenient
label and is validated instead of guessed.

Constrained measure types must agree with their dimensions. Invalid pairs
remain visible in the report but are not stored as exportable properties.
Safe repair recognizes one narrowly defined producer defect: a positive
density with exactly mass^1 and length^3 is normalized to mass^1 times
length^-3 without changing its value. The repair requires the complete unit
graph and is disabled by exact, no-repair, and strict import. No other
dimension is inferred from a human-readable label.

AP203 edition 1 does not author material, direct product-property, or
presentation requests. Such retained data is reported as unsupported rather
than silently discarded.

=== Configuration and administration

The retained graph covers supported approvals, people, organizations,
addresses, dates and times, documents, security classifications, product
categories, alternate products, usage substitutes, and effectivities. Import
preserves exact values and references; export reconstructs only records that
are legal in the selected target schema.

AP203 uses narrower *CC_DESIGN_...* assignments, closed vocabularies, and a
mandatory management closure. The exporter supplies documented deterministic
neutral values only for required ordinary closure, never to claim that an
unsupported source record was preserved. AP203 serial, dated, and lot
effectivities require complex *CONFIGURATION_EFFECTIVITY* structures. Modern
APs use broader *APPLIED_...* forms, but still differ: for example, AP214
restricts several effectivity assignments and AP203e2 has no product-category
relationship entity.

Dependencies, cycles, ambiguous identities, or assignments that violate the
target EXPRESS rules are omissions. Each retained node receives an *emitted*,
*unsupported*, or *malformed* disposition and reason in the export report.

Occurrence metadata is guarded by the current child name on its parent
combination. If an edited tree no longer matches, the values are omitted
instead of migrating to another member.

[[validation]]
== VALIDATION, REPAIR, AND REPORTING

Before import, *step-g* prints an entity census containing the instance count,
entity-keyword counts, and number of schema entity types used. Complex
instances contribute their explicit component types. This is an inventory,
not a promise of conversion support.

The import JSON report classifies each represented item as *handled*,
*preserved_invalid*, *filtered*, *intentionally_non_geometric*, *skipped*,
*malformed*, or *unsupported*. It also records schema coverage, hierarchy,
metadata graphs, repairs, inference, tolerances, budgets, timing, and reasons.

Import recovery proceeds from source-faithful conversion to bounded safe
repair. Only after the complete result fails validation may an eligible,
scale-bounded curve inference trigger a transactional whole-item rebuild. The
candidate is accepted only if the complete rebuilt B-rep passes normal
structural and solidness validation. Otherwise a completed storage-safe
invalid B-rep may be retained as a marked non-region before omission is used
as the final fallback. Preserved invalid geometry is tagged as unresolved; it
does not prove either an authoring error or an importer defect.

The export report covers every selected and reachable BRL-CAD object and every
requested metadata value. Normal export does not repair existing BRL-CAD
B-reps. Missing native and B-rep mappings are explicit omissions, never empty
STEP shapes. See *step-g*(1) and *g-step*(1) for policy controls and exit
statuses.

[[resources]]
== RECOMMENDED PRACTICES AND SCHEMA SOURCES

=== Interoperability references

EXPRESS defines legal data structures, but recommended practices establish
the entity patterns exchange systems use. Relevant references include:

* PDES, Inc., _Recommended Practices for AP203_, Recommended Practice 8,
  April 1998;
* PDM Implementor Forum, _Usage Guide for the STEP PDM Schema_, release 4.3;
* CAx Implementor Forum recommended practices and joint testing material at
  https://www.cax-if.org/joint_testing_info.html#recpracs; and
* ISO catalogue entries for
  https://www.iso.org/standard/20597.html[AP203],
  https://www.iso.org/standard/44305.html[AP203 edition 2],
  https://www.iso.org/standard/43669.html[AP214], and
  https://www.iso.org/standard/57620.html[AP242].

=== EXPRESS schema provenance

BRL-CAD treats an EXPRESS long form as generated-code input, not a generic
run-time data file. Building every schema in the STEP module library would add
substantial generation, compilation, link, and installation cost without
providing application-protocol conversion semantics. A schema is enabled only
when BRL-CAD has a selector, generated bindings with the correct physical
layout, representative inputs or fixtures, and defined conversion policy.

The authoritative source for current modular schemas is ISO's STEP Module and
Resource Library (SMRL) at
https://standards.iso.org/iso/10303/smrl/[standards.iso.org/iso/10303/smrl/].
Numbered archives such as `vN/tech/smrlvN.zip` supply published long forms;
SMRL v12 is the source used for the AP242 edition 4 binding and includes the
published AP203 edition 2 long form. BRL-CAD does not copy the archive
wholesale. Incorporated schema files must retain their applicable ISO
permission and modification notices.

The historical STEP Module Repository at
https://sourceforge.net/projects/stepmod/[sourceforge.net/projects/stepmod]
predates the ISO-hosted SMRL. Its reference area documents early CAx
Implementor Forum profiles still encountered in commercial files.
`ccd_cla_gvp_ast_asd.exp`, for example, identifies an interim AP203 extension
profile with validation and presentation additions. It is used to verify the
AP203e2 compatibility route rather than built as another exporter.

The neighboring `ap203_smch_may3_lf.exp` is a broader experimental
construction-history schema retained as a diagnostic reference. It is not a
built-in converter without representative inputs and policies for its added
feature and procedural-solid semantics. The repository's
https://sourceforge.net/p/stepmod/repo-migration/general/thread/6562cc2e/[migration discussion]
records the move to ISO-hosted archives. Historical provenance does not by
itself establish redistribution permission; applicable terms must be reviewed
before adding a schema to BRL-CAD.

Compatibility names such as `CONFIGURATION_CONTROL_3D_DESIGN_MIM_LF`,
`CCD_CLA_GVP_AST`, `CCD_CLA_GVP_AST_ASD`, and `AUTOMOTIVE_DESIGN_CC2` are
mapped only after their entity definitions and physical layouts are verified.
Otherwise the importer uses an edition-isolated binding or reports the schema
as unsupported.

=== Other application protocols

AP209 analysis, AP210 electronic assembly, AP224 machining features, and
AP238 STEP-NC share integrated resources with the supported mechanical APs,
but their domain semantics are not interchangeable. AP232 technical-data
packaging and AP239 product-life-cycle support are primarily metadata
protocols. Their schemas may inform corpus classification and future plugins;
they are not silently routed through AP203, AP214, or AP242.
