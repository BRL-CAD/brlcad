ESTABLISHED FINDINGS
--------------------

1. Commit 23eabb2c056fc7d0c4ccc6c5d9299f72eedbb432 correctly replaced the
   unsafe file-static SurfaceTree::hasSplit cache with a per-SurfaceTree
   SurfaceSplitCache.  Do not restore the static cache.

2. brep_build_bvh currently leaks each SurfaceTree.  This leak prevents
   SurfaceTree::~SurfaceTree from double-deleting its root after that root is
   attached to the master BVH.  CurveTrees likewise lack an owner after cache
   deserialization.  No repository reader of ON_BrepFace::m_face_user.p was
   found.

3. The current Newton solver fails to accept an initially converged root before
   checking its Jacobian.  Its singular "jitter" is identically zero on the
   first iteration and uses drand48.  Ev1Der/finite/normal results need checks.

4. The current fixed BREP_EDGE_MISS_TOLERANCE is used in incompatible UV and
   model-space roles.  This will be addressed only after Phase 1 behavior is
   isolated and tested.

5. The historical Cobb generator is a valid but non-solid six-face patch set
   with 24 independent edges.  libbrep now provides both that representation
   and a valid, closed, oriented variant with 8 shared vertices, 12 shared
   edges, and 24 trims.  The support surfaces are an exact sphere to sampled
   roundoff.  Controlled seam/trim perturbations are still required.

6. Primary correctness validation will pair original implicit/CSG raytracing
   with converted BREP raytracing on identical lines, including Crofton
   crossing/chord statistics.  Grazing tests will sweep signed clearance and
   verify the two-root -> tangent-contact -> no-root limit.

7. The old native ID_BSPLINE ray path dates to the 1991 Release 4.0 NURB
   module.  It was compile-time unreachable after ID_BSPLINE began converting
   to BREP in 2009, and it could not compile if re-enabled after its hit-list
   helpers were deleted in 2010.  Its live nmg_nurb_intersect engine projects
   a surface onto two ray planes, applies control-hull clipping, and recursively
   subdivides.  Those concepts are useful precedents, but the implementation
   allocates and copies surfaces during recursion, has fixed-size/order
   assumptions, stops on a UV-only tolerance, has no trim support, and the old
   wrapper fabricated a 0.01-unit exit for odd hit counts.  Do not port it.

8. The first R=10 mm grazing sweep found a deterministic but incorrect
   baseline.  As h -> 0+ the converted BREP chord stopped converging and
   approached about 0.001812758 mm.  At exact tangency it reported that false
   interval, and it reported false intervals for small negative h out to
   between approximately -7.45e-8 mm and -1.49e-7 mm.  The conditioned root
   test described in Finding 11 has repaired this baseline.  Keep the history
   as evidence for why the permanent exact-tangent and negative-h gates exist.

9. Solver work follows a corpus-first ratchet rule learned from the STEP work:
   reproduce difficult cases in C/C++ with analytic or paired implicit
   oracles, keep permanent passing gates, permit documented defects to improve
   without freezing their bad answer, and promote each repaired defect to a
   hard gate.  Avoid isolated changes tested only against their motivating
   model.

10. A ray starting exactly on the +X sphere seam and pointing outward is a
    raw miss in rt_sph_shot, which explicitly rejects an outside/on-surface
    origin pointing away.  Current BREP shot instead returns the wholly
    nonpositive interval [-2R, 0].  The in-memory corpus permits that known
    result to disappear, but requires determinism and forbids any forward
    material.  Once BREP applies the normal half-ray/partition policy, promote
    this case to exact implicit parity.

11. A fixed perpendicular line residual is not a sufficient root criterion at
    tangency.  For a unit surface normal N and ray direction D, residual divided
    by |N dot D|/|D| is the first-order error along the ray.  Root acceptance
    now requires that estimate to satisfy the numerical root tolerance, with a
    1e-13 residual floor for exact/double contact.  Residuals are evaluated
    from S-ray_origin instead of a global plane offset, avoiding cancellation
    on million-unit translated fixtures.  This retained the singular north
    pole, small/large scale, and translated cases while repairing the grazing
    limit.  This is solver conditioning, not the final contact or seam policy.

12. The correct first CSG stress test preserves the Boolean hierarchy and
    replaces only exactly convertible implicit leaves with BREP leaves.  It
    does not collapse the tree through ON_Boolean.  Disjoint and overlapping
    sphere unions, a sphere-intersection lens, a concentric subtraction
    cavity, and box-minus-cylinder all agree on every directed and shared line
    in this form.

13. A diagnostic experiment that did collapse the same binary trees to single
    evaluated BREPs separated Boolean-conversion defects from ray-solver
    defects.  Disjoint sphere union and concentric cavity happened to match,
    but overlapping union, intersection lens, and box-minus-cylinder were
    geometrically wrong before raytracing.  At 8,000 lines, overlapping union
    produced BREP area/volume about 313.2/527.7 versus implicit 497.6/937.5;
    the lens produced about 265.4/469.5 versus 121.9/105.5; and the drilled
    box produced zero BREP chord volume with invalid partitions.  Treat this
    as a later ON_Boolean/conversion qualification problem, not evidence
    against the leaf BREP intersection solver.

14. Two later-stage duality tiers are now specified in the plan: replacing
    exactly convertible leaves throughout larger existing CSG databases while
    preserving their trees, and using the experimental `brep csg`/
    brep_to_csg shape recognizer to reconstruct full or partial implicit trees
    from controlled and NIST BREP examples.  Fully implicit reconstructions
    are independent oracles; mixed reconstructions must disclose residual
    BREP leaves and make a weaker claim.

15. The openNURBS expert NewFace documentation says its vertex indices are
    ordered (SW, SE, NW, NE), but NewOuterLoop in the implementation consumes
    them in boundary order (SW, SE, NE, NW) and relates edge i to vertices i
    and (i+1)%4.  Reusing two adjacent Cobb edges exposed the inconsistency.
    Cobb construction follows the implementation order and the topology tests
    make this dependency observable.

16. The first controlled bowed-seam sweep proves that current tolerance logic
    does not reliably preserve the intended solid.  Moving only the three
    interior boundary CVs of one Cobb face inward, while preserving endpoints,

    near-grazing root.  At g/T=1 the defect persists through h/T=0.9, where the

    current T=0.0005 mm).  Normal partition assembly may then discard the
    entire interval.  The 416-ray matrix currently records 15 below-envelope
    zero-partition leaks and one forward/reverse inconsistency.  Leakage does
    not currently spread to h greater than the measured gap; that is now a
    one-way ratchet, while any repaired cases are allowed to disappear.

17. Near tangency, a surface displacement g can amplify into an along-ray
    endpoint change much larger than g, approximately through the same
    1/|N dot D| conditioning governing the solver.  Therefore seam validation
    cannot gate endpoint error directly by g.  It must distinguish geometric
    support uncertainty, root conditioning, interval existence/winding, and
    final minimum-segment policy.

21. Eligible two-trim edges can be prepared without enlarging the serialized
    cache format.  Exact-parameter NURBS forms are decomposed into nonempty

    hull a conservative curve bound.  A line within the declared edge

    produces 12 supported edges and 12 spans, and every one of the 15 current

    diagnostic rather than a proof; only box exclusion is presently claimed
    conservative.

22. Edge proximity alone is insufficient: the closest ray point must also be

    both lifts are within the then-effective edge tolerance.  A sharp convex ARB8

    252 target observations in the bowed Cobb matrix qualify: 152 inside, 32
    contact, and 68 outside; all 15 current crack leaks have valid inside-
    sector evidence.  Maximum target lift-discrepancy error is about
    2.51e-15 mm.  This remains trace-only.  General STEP use still needs
    stronger trim/edge parameter-correspondence qualification and vertex-fan
    fixtures.

23. An unresolved solid hit can be considered for topology-local closure only

    15 current below-envelope Cobb leaks with exactly one candidate: forward

24. Exact-parameter positive-weight NURBS surface spans can be prepared by the

25. For the controlled bowed seam, the existing adjacent-face root supplies a

26. Tensor-product scalar Bernstein nets are sufficient for allocation-free

    2.27e-9 T; ambiguity beyond 1e-6 T is now forbidden.  Peak sphere work is

    coefficient treatment for the continuation's outside-span extension and
    an adaptive termination/box corrector before it can replace dense trees.

27. A bounded Bezier continuation interval can be handled without assuming
    the rational convex-hull property survives arbitrary extrapolation.
    Algebraic de Casteljau reparameterization maps the requested outside-span
    rectangle back to [0,1]^2.  Its exact control-net operator infinity norm
    is computed from transformed Bernstein basis vectors and used to amplify
    all inherited coefficient error bounds.  The reparameterized weight hull
    must remain positive beyond that error or the certificate fails.  The
    normal fixed-capacity isolator then proves which UV/t boxes remain.  A
    continuation is certified only when every retained box contains the
    solved root cluster and none can also contain the existing root.  All 15
    below-envelope leaks pass.  Maximum certificate work is 93 boxes, stack
    high-water 20, and maximum retained t-cluster width about 5.15e-5 mm;
    there are no positive-weight, workspace, trace-capacity, or existing-root
    overlaps.  The certified transition pair assembles exactly one shadow
    material segment for all 15 leaks, with both endpoints within 1e-7 mm of
    the implicit sphere interval in both ray directions.  Normal segment
    emission is still unchanged.  General activation remains blocked on
    stronger trim/edge correspondence, declared/inferred tolerance policy,
    non-isoparametric and vertex cases, stable shading endpoint provenance,
    and conservative fallback integration.

28. Edge tolerance metadata must not collapse unset, explicit zero, and a

    Explicit zero remains a declaration rather than being inferred.  An unset

    a separate 32-epsilon world-coordinate floor admits only the cancellation

    uses the same scale-aware numerical allowance rather than a fixed model-
    space constant.

    On the analytic R=10 sphere, all 36 cases with chord at least T recover
    two distinct local root clusters and both analytic endpoints within
    8.56e-8 mm.  All 23 positive cases below T collapse to one contact-sized

    count alongside the edge-sector and closure classifier.  Raw numerical

    forming the same one physical cluster.  This answers the similarity

    Arbitrary nonuniform scaling is not a similarity and remains a separate

30. Local mathematical roots now feed a first allocation-free clustering
    stage rather than relying on test-side vectors alone.  A fixed insertion
    sort orders at most 64 roots by t.  The initial compatibility rule joins
    only roots on the same face whose t values are within 0.1 of the active
    model distance tolerance; each cluster records its t and normal-dot range,
    root count, entering/leaving/tangent observations, face, and preliminary
    ENTER/LEAVE/CONTACT/UNRESOLVED class.  Capacity and the provisional
    clustering tolerance are explicit trace fields.  This 0.1T value is a
    shadow rollout policy, not the final ray-interval or minimum-valid-size
    tolerance.

    Every sphere case with analytic chord at least T now produces exactly two
    ordered clusters, ENTER followed by LEAVE.  Exact tangency produces one
    CONTACT cluster.  The classifier similarity matrix gates cluster counts;
    exactly singular raw representative counts may change with floating
    conditioning, but identity, rigid, 0.01x, and 10,000x cases all retain one
    physical contact cluster.  The bowed leak matrix produces cluster-count
    histogram 14/0/1 for one/two/three clusters.  Machine output includes each
    cluster and exact attempt = unique + duplicate + failure accounting.

    This is deliberately not the final topology resolver.  Cross-face roots
    require shared-edge/same-surface-seam compatibility, trimmed candidates
    require evidence-preserving classification, clusters need certified t
    intervals rather than point-distance grouping, and minimum-segment policy
    must remain separate.  No production event or segment is emitted from
    these preliminary clusters.

31. Surface-tree leaf discovery now has an allocation-free fixed-array
    overload in shadow mode.  It traverses the existing prepared child vectors
    in exactly the same depth-first order as the std::list overload, stores up
    to 128 leaf pointers in caller-owned memory, continues counting after
    capacity, and reports overflow explicitly.  Trace-mode shooting still
    invokes the allocating traversal first and uses its list for the live
    solver loop; the fixed traversal is compared by total count and pointer
    order, so the list remains an automatic correctness fallback while the
    shadow path is qualified.

    Every traced sphere grazing/contact/miss ray, tolerance-metadata ray,
    convex/concave/periodic sector ray, classifier similarity ray, and all 416
    bowed-seam rays now hard-gate count equality, order equality, and absence
    of overflow.  The ordinary sphere grazing sweep reaches 8 intersected
    leaves, the bowed matrix reaches 12, and the translated/rotated 0.01x
    similarity reaches 68.  The latter initially exhausted a 64-entry trial
    capacity, demonstrating that geometric similarity does not imply an
    invariant dense-tree leaf count even though the physical event classifier
    remains invariant.  The retained 128-entry trace workspace is 1 KiB of
    pointers on 64-bit systems and has explicit accounting; it is not a claim
    that 128 is sufficient for arbitrary corpus models.

    This shadow checkpoint removes allocation from leaf collection itself,
    not from the complete production shot.  Hit lists, miss/trim cleanup, and
    fallback/event integration still allocate.  Any live promotion must make
    its capacity decision before partial solver or cleanup side effects.

32. After the count/order shadow gate passed, production leaf discovery was
    promoted to the 128-pointer fixed array.  Capacity is resolved before any
    surface solver or hit-cleanup state is mutated.  A ray exceeding capacity
    immediately rebuilds the legacy std::list and runs the unchanged solver
    loop from that list; a bounded ray runs the solver in the already-proven
    fixed-array order.  Trace mode deliberately builds both representations,
    reports whether fallback was selected, and continues to gate their exact
    equivalence.  All current trace fixtures take the bounded path, and the
    focused cache, analytic, Cobb, and NURBS suite remains 13/13.  A separate
    Debug build instrumented with AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehavior-
    Sanitizer also passes the complete rt_brep_ray corpus without a sanitizer
    diagnostic.  LeakSanitizer cannot run under the host ptrace wrapper and is
    explicitly not claimed by this gate.

    This promotion removes dynamic allocation from production surface-tree
    leaf collection for bounded rays.  It does not yet make BREP shooting
    allocation-free: brep_hit and cleanup structures remain std::list based,
    and an over-capacity leaf ray intentionally allocates in the safe legacy
    fallback.  Root/event conversion must preserve the same rule: decide to
    fall back before applying any partial cleanup or material-state change.

33. ThreadSanitizer qualification of parallel cache prep exposed four real
    publication races before it could validate SurfaceTree ownership.  The
    libbu small-object heap raced on its process-global lazy atexit flag, and
    bu_dir used a process-global scratch buffer even when callers supplied an
    output buffer.  Heap registration is now serialized only when a per-CPU
    size bin is initialized, and the legacy implicit bu_dir result buffer is
    thread-local.  The ordinary bu_dir and heap tests pass.

    openNURBS ONX_Model construction, archive reading/writing, and destruction
    rebuild render-setting defaults through non-reentrant localtime().  All
    ONX_Model lifetimes in the BREP primitive are now wrapped by the reserved
    datetime semaphore.  This serializes BREP archive import/export, not
    SurfaceTree construction or ray prep.  It can be narrowed or removed once
    the external OpenNURBS implementation uses localtime_r/localtime_s.

    More importantly, _rt_find_identical_solid published a soltab by setting
    st_id before primitive prep or cache deserialization completed.  A second
    tree-walk worker could therefore consume st_specific while
    rt_brep_prep_serialize was still assigning it.  soltab now has an explicit
    prep-complete publication flag protected by the same directory-hash
    semaphore used for identical-solid lookup.  Matching workers wait without
    holding the semaphore, and success or failure publishes all primitive
    pointers, bounds, and path state before reuse.  The hierarchy fixture also
    exposed the documented race caused by incrementing directory d_uses under
    different region and solid locks; both uses now share the directory-hash
    semaphore without nested lock ordering.

    The normal focused suite remains 13/13, the ASan/UBSan focused suite is
    12/12 with leak detection disabled for the host ptrace limitation, and all
    seven serial/parallel cache tests pass under ThreadSanitizer with
    halt_on_error=1 and no suppression file.  This is the first direct race
    qualification of the repaired per-BREP SurfaceTree and CurveTree ownership
    path; it does not prove unrelated librt operations outside this cache
    matrix race-free.

34. A topological edge parameter and an incident trim parameter do not in
    general have the same normalized fraction, even when both curves describe
    exactly the same locus.  The former discrepancy sampler, material-sector
    frame, and continuation seed all made that representation-dependent
    assumption.  They now map an edge point to each incident surface-lifted
    trim geometrically: an orientation-aware normalized value is only an
    initial hint, a fixed 33-sample scan finds a deterministic basin, and a
    bounded 24-step Gauss-Newton corrector with an eight-step line search
    minimizes 3D lift distance.  The composed trim/surface derivative is used
    only by correction; point-only scans avoid unnecessary derivative work.
    The routine allocates no ray-time memory and rejects invalid domains,
    topology, evaluation results, and nonfinite arithmetic.

    A copied bowed Cobb sphere converts both trims of the target edge to NURBS
    and applies opposite Möbius reparameterizations c=0.05 and c=20.  This
    changes the UV point evaluated at the trim-domain midpoint by at least
    0.452 while preserving the curve locus to 2.48e-16.  The fixture refreshes
    each OpenNURBS proxy domain after knot roundoff and requires the BREP to
    remain valid.  Against the identity case it preserves all inward,
    contact, and exterior sector states in both ray directions, measured
    discrepancy, unique closure and missing direction, continuation
    certificate, and published production interval.  The existing rotation,
    translation, 0.01x, and 10,000x similarity cases remain unchanged.

    This establishes regular two-trim correspondence invariance for the
    controlled fixture.  By itself it is not a conservative proof of the
    maximum seam discrepancy: the preparatory 33 edge samples and
    correspondence basin scan remain fixed.  Finding 35 adds a bounded shadow
    certificate for its supported regular subset; ambiguous or
    self-overlapping trims, exact-locus promotion, and vertex fans remain open
    and must retain explicit unsupported fallback.

35. A prep-time shadow pass now constructs a knot-aware conservative interval
    for the geometric gap between each supported shared 3D edge locus and
    each of its two surface-lifted trim loci.  The edge was already decomposed
    into exact-parameter positive-weight rational Bezier spans.  The new pass
    converts each trim to an exact NURBS form, splits it at every positive knot
    span, and uses the prepared exact-parameter positive-weight surface spans.
    It rejects unsupported order, weights, domains, topology, incomplete trim
    coverage, inconsistent orientation, nonfinite evaluation, and ambiguous
    endpoint correspondence instead of manufacturing a certificate.

    Each initial cell pairs one edge Bezier span with the incident trim
    interval found by the fixed geometric edge-to-lift projection.  Adjacent
    mapped intervals must be continuous and cover the complete trim domain in
    the direction required by m_bRev3d.  Child midpoints repeat the geometric
    projection inside the parent trim interval using a bounded nine-sample
    basin scan.  This preserves the nonlinear trim-reparameterization result
    without restoring the equal-normalized-speed assumption.

    Positive rational Bezier convexity supplies the actual bound.  The edge
    control points bound its Hausdorff deviation from its endpoint chord.  A
    knot-aware restricted trim control hull bounds the UV interval, and each
    overlapping prepared surface patch is restricted to that rectangle with
    fixed-array homogeneous de Casteljau operations.  The restricted positive
    surface control hull then bounds the lifted trim's deviation from its
    endpoint chord.  Exact segment Hausdorff distance plus or minus the two
    chord deviations gives a conservative cell interval; maxima combine cells
    and the two incident faces.  The UV rectangle is padded by a
    parameter-scale roundoff allowance before surface restriction.

    Cells subdivide until the aggregate interval is no wider than 0.1 model
    tolerance, with maximum depth 24.  Prep work is limited to 4,096 visited
    cells per solid.  Capacity exhaustion is recorded on both an interrupted
    edge and any later otherwise-eligible edge; no partial result is marked
    bounded.  Per-edge lower/upper limits, target width, cells, maximum depth,
    and exhaustion state are exposed in the trace and Cobb CSV.  Temporary
    vectors and Bezier objects are prep-time only; the pass adds no ray-time
    allocation.

    Scope is deliberately narrow at this checkpoint.  Exact-looking records
    at or below max(roundoff, 1e-6 T) remain unbounded because a zero fixed
    sample is not an exact-locus proof.  Records whose established sample is
    already beyond the model envelope skip the expensive upper certificate.
    The bound is shadow evidence only: discrepancy_authorized and production
    crack repair still use the pre-existing sampled policy.  Promotion needs
    complete exact-locus handling, unsupported/capacity fallback policy, and
    a tighter decision at the g=T boundary.

    The bowed Cobb target requires 2,046 cells at depth 9 and its worst final
    interval width is 0.0823 T.  It remains bounded under identity, opposite
    c=0.05/c=20 trim reparameterizations, rigid transform, 0.01x similarity,
    and 10,000x similarity with scaled model tolerances.  Every dense
    calibrated g<=T bowed case lies inside its reported interval; g>T cases
    are explicitly outside this shadow pass.  A separate three-seam matching
    fixture consumes exactly 4,096 cells, certifies two seams, and marks the
    third exhausted, proving that capacity neither truncates nor silently
    qualifies a record.

    Prep-cost experiments prevented an attractive local algorithm from
    becoming a new global cost.  Allocating OpenNURBS patch restriction in
    every cell made rt_brep_ray take 47.8 seconds; fixed homogeneous arrays
    reduced that to about 33 seconds; the 0.1 T target took 22.15 seconds.
    Skipping exact-looking records reduced it to 9.06 seconds.  Imported NIST
    geometry still exposed roughly 60 seconds of prep on many sub-tolerance
    seams; skipping already-rejected samples reduced that to 11.74 seconds,
    and the solid-wide budget reduced NIST03_MISS_01 to about 0.7 seconds.
    The current normal focused gate completes in about 10 seconds.  These are
    engineering observations on this host, not performance acceptance limits.

    compatible.

  * The closure pass runs after direction cleanup and only
    considers solid, non-plate BREPs with one unmatched final hit.  A candidate
    must be a tolerance-qualified valid inside sector, share the hit's face,

    state, unique closure count, missing enter/leave direction, and event
    ordering and the published repaired interval in both ray directions.
    BREP edge/vertex and ray model tolerances are explicitly scaled as model-
    space lengths.

  * Prepared face records now decompose exact-parameter NURBS surfaces at all
    nonempty knot rectangles into positive-weight rational Bezier patches.
    Each patch retains its original UV domain and a conservative control-hull
    bound.  Trace-mode homogeneous ray-plane coefficient hulls reject spans
    whose first or second numerator cannot contain zero.  The roundoff bound
    accounts for large-coordinate subtraction.  Cobb has six supported faces
    and six spans; the representative near-tangent line retains two spans and
    this count is unchanged by all similarity fixtures.

  * After a unique authorized closure is found, the resolver reflects the
    existing adjacent-face UV root across the associated trim tangent and run
    a fixed-budget, line-searched solve on a bounded extension of that
    prepared Bezier support.  Every current leak produces one correctly
    oriented opposite event.  The maximum endpoint error against the implicit
    sphere over all 15 is approximately 2.71e-8 mm.  Continuation attempts,
    candidate count, face/UV/t, residual, normal dot, and iteration count are

    ray-plane coefficient pair using a fixed 64-box depth-first stack and
    maximum order 16 scratch nets.  Retained depth-24 boxes include global UV
    bounds and rational-convex-hull ray-t intervals.  Every bowed-matrix raw
    Newton root must fall inside one such UV/t box; overflow or silent loss is
    a test failure.  The machine report emits every retained box.

  * The analytic sphere grazing sweep independently requires both roots to be
    covered while their chord is at least T, and requires no single box to
    contain both once the chord is at least 10T.  Exact tangency retains a
    conservative contact box but produces no final segment.  Negative-h
    ambiguity is measured on every representable mirrored sample and may not
    spread beyond 1e-6 T; the present largest is approximately 2.27e-9 T.

    prepared tree or the established tolerance policy.

    geometry.  It now holds a non-owning const pointer and copies that pointer

    debug helper, were removed along with the unused MissList declaration.

    stable insertion sort, and applies index-based equivalents of every
    legacy near-miss, near-hit, grazing, duplicate, and direction cleanup
    stage.  Solid and plate segment publication consume the fixed result
    directly.  Workspace slots are assigned in full only as hits arrive, so
    the other capacity slots are not eagerly initialized for every shot.

  * Trace mode still runs the legacy std::list path from the same solved hits.
    It bitwise-compares every raw hit after stable sorting, hard-gates the hit
    count after each cleanup stage, and compares every final hit field.  The

  * Hit-workspace overflow is detected before cleanup or segment publication.
    Production then reruns the deterministic surface solve into the legacy
    list; trace mode exposes overflow and fallback as hard-gated counters.
    Surface-leaf traversal has its independent 128-pointer fallback.  Thus the
    bounded common path avoids dynamic leaf, trim-query, raw-hit, sort, and
    cleanup containers, while unsupported capacity cases retain correctness.

  * Outside-span continuation rectangles are reparameterized algebraically
    into Bernstein form.  The code computes the absolute row-sum norm of each
    one-dimensional basis transformation, multiplies the tensor norms, and
    inflates ray-plane, ray-t, and weight errors accordingly.  A certificate
    requires the complete transformed weight hull to remain positive and the
    fixed isolator to retain only boxes belonging to the recovered root
    cluster.  No retained box may cover the original root.
