Remaining STEP Import/Export Work
=================================

Status: 2026-08-05


Purpose
-------

This is the current implementation and qualification plan for the public
step-g and g-step converters.  It replaces recommended_practices_todo.txt,
save_invalid_plan.txt, and step_refactor.txt.  It is not a development
changelog: completed work is summarized only to define the supported baseline,
and the detailed sections describe work which remains.

Status terms used below are:

* COMPLETE -- implemented and covered by focused tests;
* ACTIVE -- being qualified or reduced from real inputs;
* REMAINING -- useful work with a defined scope;
* BLOCKED -- requires a missing prerequisite and must not be approximated; and
* DEFERRED -- outside the present converter objective.

A feature is complete only when schema-valid fixtures, permissive and strict
policy tests, and structured report assertions prove that geometry or semantics
did not disappear.  Independent-producer evidence is required wherever a
BRL-CAD export and re-import could otherwise repeat the same mistake.


Supported Baseline
------------------

Architecture and schemas
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The public converter architecture is COMPLETE:

* step-g and g-step are schema-dispatching hosts;
* AP203 edition 1, AP203 edition 2, AP214 edition 3, and AP242 editions 1
  through 4 are bidirectional plugins;
* AP242 generated bindings remain edition-specific because the entity sets and
  some Part 21 physical layouts differ;
* the AP242 export alias targets edition 4, while automatic import probes
  editions 4 through 1;
* common conversion behavior is schema-neutral and schema-only behavior is
  under ap203, ap203e2, ap214, or ap242; and
* obsolete schema-specific public converter programs are not built.

Explicit --schema ap242e1 through ap242e4 selection remains useful when a file
uses only the editions' common physical subset.  Interim identifiers
CONFIGURATION_CONTROL_3D_DESIGN_MIM_LF, CCD_CLA_GVP_AST, and
CCD_CLA_GVP_AST_ASD route to AP203e2.  Supported multi-schema AP203e2 headers
also route correctly; unknown companion schemas remain visible, and genuinely
ambiguous supported-AP combinations fail rather than guessing.

The STEPcode lazy reader supports multiple DATA sections, SCOPE,
source-ordered complex components, bounded diagnostics, type census output,
and schema-independent retention of unknown configuration records.  AP203
Recommended Practice 8 fixtures cover referenced, mapped, and replicated
shapes, multi-level hierarchy, SHUO and usage semantics, multiple SDR forms,
wireframes, configuration data, and SCOPE.

STEPcode and build tractability
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

BRL-CAD uses STEPcode's compact generated API v2, packed schema modules,
precomputed attribute layouts, bounded unity chunks, and generated source
manifests.  Entity and type chunk limits are independently tunable; 64:8 is the
qualified default.  STEP plugins retain optimized compilation but disable LTO.

On the qualification host, a serial AP242e4 binding build without LTO fell
from 8m25s and 6.22 GB peak RSS at 64:64 to 6m12s and 1.33 GB at 64:8.  NIST
CTC 03 showed no measurable conversion benefit from LTO.

The importer is split into schema-neutral common build units for session and
cache handling, document traversal, job scheduling, geometric sets, topology,
periodic bands, pullback, seam repair, loop repair, validation, and
finalization.  Schema plugins consume those objects directly, preserving
static Factory registration without a new runtime ABI.  A local geometry
repair edit therefore recompiles its topical unit and relinks only requested
plugins.  Revisit these boundaries only in response to a measured dependency,
ownership, or rebuild problem.

Default policy and loss accounting
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The user-facing default is deliberately permissive:

* safe topology repair is enabled;
* exact and strict modes are disabled;
* storage-safe invalid BReps are preserved and explicitly tagged;
* useful partial output is published with status 1 and a structured report;
* --strict is transactional; and
* --reject-invalid-objs restores invalid-BRep rejection.

Recovery order is source-faithful construction, bounded safe repair, tagged
modeling inference for a proven source contradiction, marked storage-safe
invalid fallback, and finally omission.  Scale-bounded inference is attempted
only in a detached whole-item retry after the ordinary candidate fails.  It is
committed only when either:

* the rebuilt BRep passes normal structural and solidness validation; or
* permissive invalid preservation proves that a structurally safe open result
  corresponds one-for-one to source EDGE_CURVEs with exactly one authored
  ORIENTED_EDGE use.  Such a result remains invalid and is tagged
  `step:invalidity=authored_open_topology`; it is never presented as a solid.

Inference is disabled by exact, strict, no-repair, and reject-invalid policy.
Reports and affected database objects retain source edge identifiers,
discrepancy, safe and inference limits, declared tolerance, inference kind,
and explanatory detail.  Rejected inference transactions do not contaminate
ordinary results or diagnostics.

Every product-bound representation, representation item, selected BRL-CAD
object, and reachable child receives a handled, intentionally non-geometric,
skipped, malformed, unsupported, or failed disposition.  Import and export
distinguish complete, partial, empty, and failed outcomes.  The pre-conversion
entity census describes the input and does not imply conversion support.

A preserved invalid BRep is not, by itself, proof of bad authoring.  Unless an
independent topology proof establishes a source contradiction, it remains an
unresolved converter result tagged `step:geometry_status=invalid_preserved`
and `step:source_validity=unresolved`.  It forces a partial result and remains
in the failure ratchet.

Geometry
~~~~~~~~

The following geometry paths are COMPLETE for their documented subsets:

* rigid product hierarchies, repeated occurrences, transforms, and shared
  product/occurrence emission in every supported schema;
* optional native CSG import/export in AP203e2, AP214, and AP242, including
  supported Boolean trees, exact mapped primitives, BRep leaves, transforms,
  and RECTANGULAR_PYRAMID;
* exact compatible extruded and revolved area/face solids and linear or
  circular SWEPT_DISK_SOLID in AP203e2, AP214, and AP242;
* AP242 triangulated faces, simple/complex surface sets, shells, and solids,
  including strips, fans, pnindex, normals, welding, closure, and winding;
* analytic and spline BRep import, periodic and singular topology, voids,
  bounded repair, transactional inference, and explicit invalid fallback;
* solid-BRep export/re-import for sphere, cylinder, cone, torus, box, open
  NURBS faces with voids, rational Cobb surfaces, and BREP_WITH_VOIDS;
* mated librt BRep fallback for EHY, EPA, HYP, PART, PIPE, EXTRUDE, REVOLVE,
  solid BOT, closed ARS, and supported NMG input;
* sketch recovery for reordered or reversed segments, zero-length segments,
  inner loops, lines, circles, arcs, Beziers, and rational/non-rational NURBS;
  and
* mixed GEOMETRIC_SET curves, points, and CURVE_BOUNDED_SURFACEs with partial
  set loss accounting and shared bounded pullback validation.

The detailed native-CSG primitive and operator matrix is maintained in
src/conv/step/README.csg.md.  AP203e1 has no native-CSG path; non-union AP203e1
trees are BLOCKED on reliable Boolean evaluation.

STEP B-spline curves and surfaces share exact conversion from EXPRESS full knot
vectors to OpenNURBS endpoint-truncated storage.  Valid unclamped closed
surfaces are clamped and only numerically equal seam controls are normalized,
subject to dense before/after audits.

Presentation, units, configuration, and PMI
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

AP203e2, AP214, and AP242 share colour, transparency, style, layer, material,
property, validation-value, and recursive unit-graph extraction.  Export
round-trips product identity and revision, presentation, multiple materials,
typed properties, known SI and exact conversion units, derived units,
occurrence labels, and reference designators.  AP203e1 explicitly reports
presentation or property requests it cannot author.

One constrained safe-mode repair handles an identified producer defect where
a positive density's complete unit graph is mass^1 times length^3 instead of
mass^1 times length^-3.  The value is retained, the denominator dimension is
corrected, provenance is recorded, and exact, strict, and no-repair modes
reject the graph.  This is not a general unit-guessing rule.

--output-units, --angle-units, and --uncertainty form one schema-neutral export
policy covering BReps, native CSG, cone angles, and rigid occurrence
translations.

Configuration data is retained as a typed reference graph with exact Part 21
right-hand sides.  Cross-schema authoring covers approvals, provenance,
assignments, effectivity, alternatives, substitutes, categories, people,
organizations, addresses, security classifications, documents, date/time
chains, and their legal schema-specific assignment forms.

DATUM supports point, line, plane, frame, and bounded-target forms, semantic
roles, axes, dimensions, identifiers, validation, transforms, and versioned
legacy-compatible database storage.  ANNOT supports model-space planes,
per-segment roles and styles, UTF-8 text, runtime TrueType outlines,
transforms, and legacy screen-space compatibility.  The hardened private
struetype code is shared from libbu; LGPL OSIFont remains runtime data and is
not compiled into a binary.

AP242 PMI stage 1 retains connected semantic, presentation, association, and
dependency records with source values, component order, references, product
ownership, and native disposition.  Exactly resolved point, axis, and plane
datums become DATUM objects.  Supported graphical content becomes model-space
ANNOT geometry; ambiguous content remains semantic-only.  Malformed
presentation is partial in permissive mode and transactional in strict mode.

Safety and test infrastructure
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

BRL-CAD and STEPcode support ASan+UBSan and a separate TSan mode.  Release and
ASan+UBSan have covered the complete STEP/AP range; focused parallel, failure,
native-CSG, and BRep-export paths have run under TSan.  On the qualification
host TSan must run through `setarch x86_64 -R` because its runtime otherwise
fails before main.  Repeated -j8 versus -j1 output comparison remains part of
the determinism gate.


Validation Snapshot
-------------------

In-tree tests include success, malformed, partial, strict, round-trip,
sanitizer, and output-validity cases across every supported schema.  The last
complete focused Release STEP matrix contains 197 tests.  Official NIST MBE PMI
files are stored under db/nist with documented provenance.  Five AP203 MIM
files exercise AP203e2, and 17 AP242 files span editions 1 through 4.

Checksum-pinned, non-redistributed STEP Tools and STEPcode samples cover CATIA,
Unigraphics, AutoCAD, I-DEAS, STEPnet, and STEP Tools producers.  Important
independent modern-schema evidence currently includes:

* AP242 Leopard2-A6: 31/31 geometry items, 14 products, 338 occurrences,
  2,425 styles, 13 material identities, and 26 material values;
* AP242 Open Source Rover: six product levels, 713 products, 1,390 rigid
  occurrences, and 569 applied styles; and
* AP203e2 ROTATOR_ASM: eight product levels, 126 participating products, 404
  usages, 671 product-property records, and 3,991 styles.

The last complete unified corpus baseline is the 1,138-file dry run at
`.build/step-corpus-full-j8-validity-ladder-20260804-post-speaker`:

* schemas: 616 AP203, 375 AP214, four AP203e2, and 143 AP242 files;
* files: 1,057 clean; process statuses were 1,064 complete, 69 useful partial,
  three intentionally empty, one failed, and one SIGSEGV;
* geometry: 41,765 of 41,992 attempted items written (99.46 percent), 221 exact
  omissions, and six unsupported-representation omissions;
* invalid fallback: 386 structurally invalid but storage-safe BReps preserved,
  none rejected;
* aggregate wall/conversion time: 36,796.2/34,678.9 seconds; and
* maximum per-file RSS: 3.62 GB.

This remains the comparison baseline, not the current expected result.  The
crash and several omission groups have since been fixed in selected working
runs.  Its timing delta from the preceding baseline is not a controlled
regression result because host load and available memory differed materially.
Preserve both manifests and compare future runs only under similar load.

The corpus runner records hashes, schemas, converter/plugin/shared-library
identities, reports, logs, wall time, peak RSS, host load, memory availability,
geometry outcomes, hierarchy depth, metadata, and failure categories.  Dry
mode still parses, constructs, validates, and repairs geometry; it omits only
database serialization.  Historical `.g` files are comparative artifacts, not
correctness oracles, and must remain intact.

Selected post-baseline qualifications establish:

* cargo ship now terminates normally; its two remaining selected omissions are
  explicit missing required FACE_SURFACE references;
* hAVYARD now terminates normally after rejected seam transactions reacquire
  topology owned by the restored BRep; a direct owned lifetime fixture remains
  to be added;
* SNAP #801290 is a valid solid after a face-scoped horn-torus keyhole repair;
* all ten historical T90 omissions serialize: six valid solids, two
  source-open BReps tagged `authored_open_topology`, and two storage-safe
  partial fallbacks whose contradictions exceed bounded inference limits;
* paired 135-file AP203/AP242 Recurta runs write all 139 attempted items per
  schema as valid BReps, with three tagged inferences per schema; and
* the selected 421-face speaker solid now produces a validated 419-face solid
  through a whole-item shared-spur transaction.

The T90 serialized selection is recorded in
`.build/t90_omissions_final_write.json`; it attempted and wrote ten of ten,
skipped none, and explicitly preserved four invalid objects.  The file-level
partial status is intentional and does not represent an omission.

Two measured performance targets remain useful:

* Recurta `10012.step`, 992 faces: 78.7 seconds in the final qualification;
  profiling attributes most time to periodic trim-orientation repair and
  repeated transaction work, not preflight construction; and
* chess king, 13,234 faces: about 616.8 seconds and 2.92 GB in its selected
  clean run.


Remaining Work in Recommended Order
-----------------------------------

1. Close the current corpus ratchet -- ACTIVE
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Complete selected work before starting another expensive unified run:

1. Add a compact rejected-seam-transaction lifetime fixture, qualify it under
   ASan+UBSan, and retain the complete hAVYARD real-file check.
2. Sample and reduce the largest post-baseline omission sets:

   * Garrett: 49;
   * 2-litre engine: 49, including 48 GEOMETRIC_SET items;
   * Open Source Rover: 16;
   * Epoxy granite VMC: seven; and
   * BLANCO: five.

3. Retest the former 58-omission GEOMETRIC_SET group with the completed bounded
   surface path.  The independent b/c/d compatibility files are already
   qualified except for six d-wireframe boundary/pcurve disagreements.
4. Reduce the Leopard2-A6 export/re-import failure at STEP edge #460691.  One
   of 31 exported BReps fails while splitting a pcurve whose candidate
   fragment is 36.4476 model units from its subedge.  This is a geometry
   interoperability problem, not schema selection or assembly loss.
5. Retain MOUSE as a source edge/surface mismatch unless independent geometry
   proves a narrower correction.
6. Revisit T90 #20240 and #20307 only if independent geometry supports a
   narrower reconstruction.  Their marked partial results are preferable to
   inventing a false solid.
7. Remove repeated full-BRep scans and copies from large periodic repair
   transactions while preserving exact rollback behavior.
8. Run focused Release, ASan+UBSan, TSan where applicable, and -j1/-j8
   determinism gates.  Then run the complete 1,138-file qualification.

Corpus fixes require a compact reproducer, permissive and strict assertions,
structured report coverage, and a selected real-file check.  Do not introduce
broad repair heuristics solely to improve corpus percentages.

The next unified run is acceptable only if every file reaches a normal terminal
report; there is no crash, hang, schema-load failure, corrupt output, or
unexplained zero-geometry result; and all omissions and invalid fallbacks are
grouped by owned root cause.

2. Expand independent AP203e2/AP242 evidence -- REMAINING
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Locate and pin independent examples for:

* AP203e2 material and property association variants;
* AP242 materials beyond the Inventor MAKE_FROM_USAGE_OPTION pattern;
* mixed representation contexts and large multiple-product files; and
* AP203e2/AP242 sweeps, NURBS, shells, voids, and mixed BRep/CSG assemblies.

Promote small distributable reductions into in-tree fixtures.  For external
inputs record source, producer, SHA-256, and structured expectations.  A
BRL-CAD-authored round trip alone cannot close this item.

3. Complete AP242 semantic PMI and MBD -- REMAINING
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Stage 1 graph retention and exact native point/axis/plane/curve creation are
complete.  Remaining interpretation and authoring work is to:

* normalize dimension type/value, tolerance type and magnitude,
  material-condition modifiers, datum-reference compartments, and zones;
* preserve stable product, occurrence, face, edge, and vertex target links;
* distinguish semantic-only, graphical-only, and validated combined PMI;
* map representable text, leaders, extension/dimension lines, arrowheads,
  fills, area targets, and supported symbols;
* explicitly report graphical content which cannot be mapped faithfully;
* author retained semantic and supported presentation records in AP242; and
* qualify against NIST PMI and independent AP242 files, not only hand-authored
  fixtures.

An ANNOT outline is not proof that original text or tolerance semantics were
recovered.  Do not infer missing semantics from graphical similarity.

4. Complete tessellated coverage and optional export -- REMAINING
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Add faithful mappings for tessellated point, curve, wire, geometric-set, and
repositioned-item forms.  Add explicit optional AP242 BOT export using the
shared unit and uncertainty policy.  Tessellation must not silently replace
the eventual evaluated-BRep default for analytic solids.

5. Broaden existing-BRep interoperability -- REMAINING
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Reduce the six d-wireframe boundary/pcurve disagreements and independently
classify each as an authored tolerance violation or adapter defect.  Preserve
faithful partial-set output until that proof exists; do not stretch pcurves or
widen model uncertainty merely to reach complete counts.

Expand independent-producer coverage for rational and non-rational NURBS,
open shells, nested BREP_WITH_VOIDS, reversed proxies/faces, difficult trims,
periodic and singular surfaces, and closed curves.  Define a legal policy for
one BRL-CAD object containing disconnected material volumes.  Add wireframe
and surface-curve export for databases without face topology.

Verification must include STEPcode readback, strict BRL-CAD re-import,
topology and material-ray comparison, and an independent STEP validator or
consumer where available.  Textual identity is insufficient.

Sketch containment still needs a complete loop hierarchy for disjoint outer
regions and nested material islands.  REVOLVE should split profiles at sharp
C0/tangent discontinuities when needed to avoid pathological wireframe and CDT
refinement.

DSP, deprecated HF, EBM, and VOL are a separate scalability and fidelity
project.  They require bounded-error patching or faithful faceting, discrete
boundary simplification, source-semantic ray/material comparisons, and hard
validity, memory, and complexity limits.  A single high-degree DSP surface or
unbounded voxel-to-BRep expansion is not an acceptable general solution.

6. Complete product and configuration mappings -- REMAINING
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Define scoped mappings and loss policy for region identifiers,
material/shader strings, and selected arbitrary BRL-CAD attributes.  Do not
conflate opaque attributes with typed STEP properties.

Broaden normalized named-unit conveniences only where schemas agree; the
recursive retained graph remains authoritative.  Extend authoring for
remaining configuration identities, relationships, complex addresses, and
application edges only when required source values exist.  Expand
import-export-import tests to multiple products, occurrences, shared complex
effectivities, and schema-specific graphs.

7. Resolve assembly and native-CSG edge cases -- REMAINING
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Define explicit policy for scale, mirror, and future non-rigid occurrence
transforms.  Preserve or explicitly report nested combination identity inside
one CSG expression; STEP CSG_SELECT cannot directly use another CSG_SOLID as
an operand.  Expand stable naming and independent multi-level mixed BRep/CSG
evidence across AP203e2, AP214, and AP242.

NOT and guard/no-op Boolean nodes have no lossless finite STEP CSG encoding.
AP242 CYCLIDE_SEGMENT_SOLID remains unsupported because recognizing a faithful
BRL-CAD PIPE subset requires additional geometric semantics; similar
dimensions alone are insufficient.

8. Implement remaining exact sweeps -- REMAINING
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Add nonlinear swept-disk directrices only when OpenNURBS can construct a
faithful pipe with validated frame continuity and self-intersection handling.
Ellipse, spline, and composite-curve cases remain.  Implement drafted and
trimmed face sweeps only after referenced termination, offset, trim intent,
and per-edge draft can be evaluated exactly.  Inherited depth or angle is not
a faithful fallback.


Blocked and Deferred Work
-------------------------

Evaluated-BRep default export -- BLOCKED
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The broadly interoperable default should eventually evaluate arbitrary
Boolean trees into final BReps.  Current Boolean evaluation is not reliable
enough.  Continue evaluator work and validation; when it is ready, make
evaluated BRep the default without weakening optional exact native CSG.

STEPcode upstreaming -- DEFERRED maintenance
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The generated-binding scalability work required by step-g and g-step is
complete.  Remaining STEPcode work is upstream preparation and maintenance:
retain API v1 compatibility where required, and address correctness, cache,
generation, or resource defects when measured.  Reopen generator scalability
only for a demonstrated regression or a new schema exceeding current limits.

Additional application protocols -- DEFERRED
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Do not build every ISO SMRL schema speculatively.  AP224 machining features and
AP238 STEP-NC are the strongest future geometry/manufacturing candidates once
representative inputs and mapping goals exist.  AP209 analysis and AP210
electronics require domain-specific semantics.  AP232 and AP239 are primarily
metadata protocols and are not geometry targets merely because they share
integrated resources.

ISO SMRL and historical STEPmod selection, provenance, and redistribution
policy are documented in STEP(5).


Definition of Done
------------------

Every new decoder, emitter, or repair must include, as applicable:

1. an independent hand-authored or external fixture;
2. a supported case and a malformed or explicitly unsupported case;
3. permissive and transactional strict assertions;
4. structured report checks proving exhaustive loss accounting;
5. schema-specific coverage where legal alternatives or physical layouts
   differ;
6. round-trip geometry, topology, semantic, and unit checks;
7. Release, sanitizer, and deterministic parallel tests;
8. selected corpus reruns for every affected real file; and
9. matching step-g, g-step, STEP(5), CSG matrix, and plan documentation.

The overall STEP effort is broadly robust when:

* every input and selected output object is exhaustively accounted for;
* the unified corpus has no crash, hang, schema-load failure, corrupt output,
  or unexplained zero-geometry result;
* AP203e2 and AP242 have independent producer evidence comparable to the
  relevant AP203/AP214 baseline;
* supported tessellation, rigid assemblies, presentation, configuration, and
  PMI round-trip with explicit losses;
* existing BReps interoperate across analytic, NURBS, periodic, singular,
  shell, void, orientation, and trim cases with independent consumers;
* native CSG remains optional, exact, and explicit about unsupported content;
* parallel, sanitizer, deterministic-output, performance, and memory gates
  remain green; and
* reliable Boolean evaluation enables evaluated-BRep default export without
  regressing native CSG.
