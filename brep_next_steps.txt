`e7c61497df`.  The accuracy and memory conclusions are unchanged: the GTA02

and 1.908x wall relative to current main, with exact routed/legacy result
agreement.  Its paired routed/legacy time medians are 1.1007x CPU and 1.0918x

2026-10-07 STAGED-CERTIFICATION RESULT AND CONCRETE RELEASE PLAN

The routing redesign is now demonstrated on both the 63-face fixture and the
complete GTA02 top-level assembly.  Production still uses a shallow depth-3
SurfaceTree.  Direction-dependent grazing proof begins with the established
256-box fixed workspace and, only when ray-visible work remains unresolved,
continues the same traversal toward a 1,024-box hard limit.  It neither
restarts the proof nor allocates during shooting.  An explicit budget of 256
or less retains the old one-stage fail-closed behavior.

At 128 square only 2 of 15,377 probes continue, consuming 46 additional boxes;
both resolve.  At 1,024 square only 120 of 987,879 probes continue.  Those
continuations consume 5,853 boxes and resolve 247 spans; six spans remain
ambiguous, trigger conservative fallback, and no workspace overflows.  This
is the intended direction-dependent refinement, not a return to uniform depth
8.  The routed image remains
`FD626C13AF6A8171DD405DE5044949903A0681CDAD144F152A6D1159B15143D4`.

The fixed five-ray GTA oracle now gives each endpoint a window no wider than
3e-5.  Every depth-3 legacy ray misses in both directions, every depth-8
reference finds one interval, and the production level-1 replay publishes the
same faces and interval with zero reversal mismatch.  The largest observed
reference/replay endpoint difference is 7.02e-8.  This converts the five
publications from an inference based on image differences into explicit,
repeatable ray evidence.

The final 63-face seven-trial archive reports 441,505.68 CPU and 432,887.67
wall rays/second for routed execution, 486,296.12/475,761.73 for branch legacy,

Measured peak working sets at 0, 8, and 64 MiB scene-pack budgets are 1.532,

2026-10-06 WEIGHTED-CACHE QUALIFICATION AND REMAINING RELEASE PLAN

The bounded caching redesign is implemented and qualified locally.  One
scene-wide 8 MiB pack payload is now distributed by deterministic projected-
area-weighted water filling, then each object admits its largest projected
correction cells first.  It remains depth/format safe, prep-built, immutable,
optional, and allocation-free during shooting.  Weighted 8 MiB cuts the GTA
128-square missing-root certificate from 64.212 to 30.900 milliseconds versus
equal sharing and beats the equal-share 64 MiB result of 40.065 milliseconds.
The final 63-face route is 1.91 times clean main and has 8.8 percent time
overhead versus branch legacy with exact routed/legacy output.  On GTA, five
certified replay completions recover five intersections and one independently
confirmed visible pixel, while the latest loaded-host series is 20.9 percent
CPU / 25.2 percent wall above branch legacy.

   configured payload ceiling, per-object eligible-byte caps, stable remainder
   ordering, projected-area cell priority, sub-minimum early exit, checked
   arithmetic, and exact packless fallback.  Do not restore independent
   per-object allowances.  Keep reporting assigned and actually constructed
   bytes separately; neither number includes vector/allocator overhead.

SUPERSEDED BROAD-CLASSIFIER FEASIBILITY DECISION
-------------------------------------------------

Near-main speed is not reachable by event micro-optimization alone.  The
current median gap is about 92x, and main's 128-square timing is coarse enough
that the true ratio may be larger or smaller.  In the controlled profile,
span discovery/isolation alone takes 4.931 seconds; deleting the entire 1.270-
second event phase would still leave throughput tens of times below main.
Reaching the 95-percent gate therefore requires avoiding or radically
shortening most failed proof attempts, not another small constant-factor win.

There is no evidence of a fundamental geometry or legacy-raytracer limit.  The
branch legacy-only path is already competitive with or faster than main, and
on the 63-face fixture it has the exact same digest as the prepared path.  The
current structural blocker is the policy of attempting exhaustive interval
isolation before discovering that 3,894 rays require legacy fallback.

The preferred architecture is now legacy-seeded and independently certified.
Run the fixed-workspace legacy solve once and retain its complete transaction.
Use its roots only as seed coordinates.  A separate verifier must establish
candidate-span coverage, unique local roots, trim and direction, complete root
count, ordered alternating material intervals, and publication data derived
from certified boxes.  On success it may replace the transaction atomically;
on any failure it returns the already complete legacy transaction.  There is
never a prepared prefix, legacy suffix, or raw legacy root promoted as proof.

This architecture can remove the present 92x failure-tax, but the measured
verifier cannot run on every candidate ray and satisfy the 95-percent gate.
Seven fresh post-packed-input trials put the full 134-ray shadow verifier at a
7.8772 ms median (7.7349--8.4229 ms).  Coefficient construction is 0.6143 ms,
about 74.7 percent below its earlier 2.431 ms median.  Removing the old
comparison-only complement stage estimates the new degree path at 4.2621 ms,
or 31.81 microseconds per audited ray.  A timer-stable 512-square seven-trial
endpoint comparison measures branch legacy-only at 479,349 rays/CPU-second
median versus main at 229,825, or 208.6 percent; the corresponding per-render-
ray costs are about 2.09 and 4.35 microseconds.  Thus legacy tracing is not the
barrier.  Spending all headroom down to the 95-percent-of-main gate would
optimistically allow the verifier on about 7.8 percent of render rays (about
5.3 percent when the aggregate verifier cost is charged only across the 90
substantive seeded attempts).  The deliberately stricter five-percent
incremental-overhead budget allows only about 0.68 percent.  Dispatch and
publication costs reduce both limits.  The render-wide profile now measures
6,305 seeded attempts among 16,384 frame rays (38.48 percent), costing
111.594--116.059 microseconds each.  Even deleting all complement work leaves
65.065--67.351 microseconds per attempt, while the 95-percent-of-main headroom
permits about 6.48 microseconds at that attempted fraction.  This rejects the
current classifier and shows that face-level risk union is too broad: 94.09 percent of
BREP shots inherit `UNQUALIFIED` plus topology-boundary risk.  The 61 complex
shadows also average 2.151--2.207 ms each and contribute 8.01--8.22
microseconds per frame ray at their present population.  Thus generic
ambiguity cannot automatically authorize the exhaustive prepared path; that
cost must be limited to corpus-proven correction classes or removed as well.

The redesign remains realistic only if a truth corpus shows that actual
accuracy corrections occupy sparse strata which cheap prep-time and ray-local
feature predicates can observe.  Qualified clean ordinary rays then go
directly to the retained legacy transaction; only observable correction-risk
rays pay the independent verifier; true boundary/seam/singular ambiguity goes
to the existing complex path.  If corrections also occur among otherwise
indistinguishable clean ordinary rays, selective routing cannot solve the
problem by itself.  That case requires roughly a tenfold faster amortized or
ray-bundle certificate, a relaxed speed goal, or retaining legacy authority
for the class.  Fully certifying every ray while matching the legacy heuristic
within five percent remains unsupported and should not be promised.

Production activation is gated on two facts not yet established: every known
legacy/prepared disagreement must either be corrected by the new independent
certificate or rejected, and a timer-stable render must reach at least 95
percent of main.  In addition, no known correction ray may be classified
`LEGACY_DIRECT`, and seeded attempts plus dispatch cost must fit the aggregate
budget.  Until all pass, the verifier remains shadow-only and the current
production authority remains unchanged.


WORKING RULES
-------------

1. Correctness remains fail-closed.  Never turn an unresolved event into a
   miss, mix a prepared prefix with a legacy suffix, or widen a tolerance to
   make a benchmark pass.
2. Measure normal production separately from trace/audit mode.  Trace mode may
   deliberately execute both prepared and legacy work.
3. Compare identical geometry, rays, compiler mode, CPU count, cache state,
   and output checks.  Report medians and ranges, not a single favorable run.
4. Make one performance hypothesis per change and retain telemetry proving
   which work was removed.  Keep accuracy and reversal gates green at every
   checkpoint.
5. Do not change the production SurfaceTree depth from eight until Milestone 5
   passes.  Cached depth variants make the experiment safe; they do not prove
   that a shallower tree is correct.
6. Treat the primitive-queried cache variant as part of every prep-option
   change.  Before enabling a new option in cached production, encode it in the
   variant, record enough payload metadata to reject a mismatch, and add a
   coexistence/reload test analogous to the depth-three/depth-eight test.
